Research

Engineering Biology for Sustainable Production

Our lab focuses on designing and optimizing microbial cell factories through metabolic engineering, synthetic biology, and systems biology approaches.

Microbial Cell Factory

Engineering E. coli, yeast, and non-model microorganisms as efficient platforms for biosynthesis of target molecules through rational and evolutionary strategies.

E. coli Yeast Fungi

Biocatalysis and Biosynthesis

Engineering microbes to produce isoprenoids, food ingredients, flavours and fragrances, specialty chemicals from renewable carbon sources.

Terpenoids Specialty chemicals Biofuels

Synthetic Pathway Design

Designing, constructing, and optimizing novel biosynthetic pathways using DNA assembly tools, protein engineering, and computational pathway design frameworks.

Pathway design Protein engineering Computation

Genomic Editing & Omics

Applying CRISPR-based genome editing, omics technologies, and AI-guided modeling to understand and rewire cellular metabolism for precision strain engineering.

CRISPR Flux analysis AI-guided design

Waste Valorisation

Developing microbial and enzymatic platforms to upcycle C1 & C2 feedstocks (methanol, CO2, ethylene glycol) and plastic waste (PET) into value-added chemicals and food ingredients.

C1/C2 feedstocks PET upcycling Circular economy

Novel Bioprocess & Scale-up

Translating laboratory-scale microbial production to pilot and industrial bioreactor conditions through fermentation process optimization and techno-economic analysis.

Fermentation Bioprocess Scale-up

ZHANG Congqiang (Simon) 张聪强

Principal Investigator

Singapore Institute of Food and Biotechnology Innovation (SIFBI), A*STAR

Dr. Zhang Congqiang (Simon) is a Principal Investigator at the Singapore Institute of Food and Biotechnology Innovation (SIFBI), A*STAR, where he leads a team in synthetic biology and metabolic engineering. His research focuses on engineering microbial cell factories for sustainable production of chemicals, fuels, food ingredients, and natural products.

He received his undergraduate and master's training in chemical engineering at Tianjin University, China, and completed his PhD in Chemical and Pharmaceutical Engineering in a joint programme between the National University of Singapore (NUS) and Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT). He has co-authored over 40 papers and holds 10+ international patents. He serves as Associate Editor for Advanced Biotechnology and Frontiers in Bioengineering and Biotechnology, a guest editor for Scientific Report and Food Wellness, and is Secretary of the BioEnergy Society of Singapore.
